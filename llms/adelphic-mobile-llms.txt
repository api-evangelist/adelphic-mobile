# Adelphic (Viant DSP)

> Adelphic is an enterprise demand-side platform (DSP) for programmatic advertising, originally founded as a mobile-first programmatic buying platform and acquired by Viant Technology in 2017. It lets marketers plan, buy, and measure omnichannel campaigns across Connected TV, linear TV, mobile, desktop, streaming audio, and digital out-of-home from a single self-service interface, using Viant's people-based Household ID for identity resolution.
